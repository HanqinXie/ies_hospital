# V23 engineering assumptions and scope

V23 inherits the original DOE/IDF/8760 demand, plant capacities, P0–P3 classifications, numerical materiality criteria, tariffs and finite storage efficiencies. The authoritative task is USER_TASK_20261025.txt. New physical execution hooks are opt-in M23; old M22 result files remain historical evidence.

* BOILER_GT_START_AC is the descriptive alias for STATION_AC. This branch feeds boiler auxiliary/restart and GT startup. It is not a new whole-plant grid fault. EC/ABS/tower cooling remains conditional on its own hardware/flow and actual common source power. Building fans and pumps retain the original service dependency ledger. No OEM topology is claimed.
* Boiler station 0.5 kWe, 0.012 kWe/kW steam and continuous one-minute paid restart are inherited explicit research assumptions. Boiler and GT start permission is not actual power. Loss of functional source/device qualification cancels accumulated continuous boiler restart time; it does not invent permanent damage or a manufacturer reset requirement.
* The existing finite DC qualification (1440 minutes, two qualified DG cranks) is a research abstraction. Actual crank consumes one token once; queued/cancelled electrical dispatch is distinct. Hardware restoration neither refunds a token nor recharges qualification. It is not an OEM starter-battery autonomy result.
* M23 boiler dispatch is an arrived conditional ceiling, whereas M22 used a fixed steam setting or trip. All strategies use the same M23 interface, original 15-minute decision clock and command queue. Preparation uses current demand only. This common engineering correction is distinct from the policy preparation/reserve factorial. No future fault, repair or outage-end time is an online observation.
* Empty cold storage with a continuing material critical-cold deficit idles rather than selecting recharge. Actual post-repair surplus may recharge; no higher empty threshold, capacity change, energy deletion or free recovery is introduced. Inventory and actual power are solved from the same snapshot before one physical commit.
* Recovery evaluation keeps original raw half-open intervals, requests, disconnected/connected deficits and ENS. Electricity has no additional sustained-restoration delay; heat/cold require a continuous 60 seconds of nonmaterial shortage. This fixed research definition represents sustained thermal flow at the original bookkeeping scale, not a clinical threshold. Confirmation sensitivity 30/120 seconds is diagnostic. The 1-second comparison band is not a physical or recovery criterion.
* Cost precision is max(0.01 USD2001, one ppm of the larger total), CO2 max(0.01 kg, one ppm), and each terminal store max(0.001 kWh, one ppm of capacity/initial shaft budget). No terminal fuel value or salvage price is invented. These are interpretation bands only; original raw amounts remain exported.
* The new temperature model has an air state and a slow mass state. Calibration parameters were frozen before reading the new validation dates. All formal native EnergyPlus inputs use the original ten-minute timestep and exactly unchanged component autosizing values. It did not meet every original validation target. Temperature and its active recovery are exploratory energy-interface diagnostics, not clinical safety evidence, and cannot cancel cooling ENS.
* Room-rate units are per representative zone; OR2 has multiplier five. Complete P0 demand includes OTHER. Allocation weights multiply each zone once, and delivery back to a representative zone divides once. Cold-service delivery excludes separately metered extra recovery cooling, pump and fan electricity.
* Active cooling recovery has a current-state one-hour recovery horizon and 100 kW request ceiling. These only define the optional exploratory request, not a new cold source or physical capacity. Real EC/source/flow/arrived permissions and all original service locks limit actual delivery. Recovery pump/fan factors 0.02/0.10 kWe per kW cooling are explicit inherited diagnostic assumptions requiring engineering measurements for deployment.
* Temperature-only January22 calibration overlaps an old sealed IES calendar day; no sealed IES episode or historical result was opened for that calibration. The complete winter IES diagnostic instead uses February19 reference rates (hour1187), with no refit. Existing IES backgrounds and the few new coupled diagnostics are not a new blind or probabilistic reliability sample.

Failures, checked-domain exhaustion, solver errors, numerical corrections, prefixes and unknown tails remain in the attempt records. Small off/run domain closure is not a proof of global feasibility for all possible plant topologies or unregistered equipment operating minima.
