# V20 design decisions

The user's task is the approved scope and design authority. Its explicit continuous-work authorization replaces older phase stops and the V19 immutable-current-tree rule for this new isolated version. Original evidence remains immutable in the V19 tag and full source workspace.

Three implementation approaches were considered: rewrite CSVs; patch only presentation tables; or correct the grouping interface under a new model identity. The interface correction is selected because CSV byte provenance remains intact and the actual dispatch consumes the corrected demands. Editing CSVs would obscure the source boundary; presentation-only changes would leave physical requests wrong.

Heating and cooling decisions require their own export/SQL evidence and must not inherit the electric decision by analogy. If evidence remains incomplete, register the specific gap and continue established corrections without inventing calibration. No proportional clipping is introduced to conceal a new excess; existing compression, if retained for compatibility, is reported before and after with its cause and hours.

The final current tree describes V20 only. V19 results remain reachable by tag/history and on local complete evidence; small inherited modules/configurations may retain legacy paths when required by actual calls. Their presence does not make a V20 result a V19 reproduction.
