Received in revised form 21 March         are typically those of the evaporator and condenser, which are difﬁcult to model. Accuracy
2008                                and execution speed of system models therefore are highly dependent on the modeling ap-
Accepted 24 March 2008                  proach of the heat exchangers. The two common approaches are the ﬁnite-volume (FV)

                                             in the literature, but there is little discussion presented for either choice. This paper pres-
Keywords:                                 ents the development and comparative study of shell-and-tube heat exchanger dynamic
Heat exchanger                        models using both the FV and the MB approaches. Detailed model formulations are pro-

Condenser                                   centrifugal chiller test stand. The FV formulation is found to be more robust through
Comparison                                start-up and  all load-change transients, but executes slower. The moving-boundary
Process                             method can handle all load-change transients but start-up stability is more sensitive to
